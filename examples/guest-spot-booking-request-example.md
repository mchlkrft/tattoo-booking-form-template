# Guest Spot Booking Request Example

An example of a complete tattoo guest spot booking request from a fictional client. The artist below is running a guest spot trip in Berlin from April 15 to April 22.

---

## Submitted request

**Trip selection**

- City of the guest spot: Berlin
- Trip dates: April 15 to April 22

**Contact details**

- Name or preferred handle: Lukas
- Instagram handle: @lukas.tattoo.client
- Email: lukas@example.com

**Tattoo idea**

- In your own words: A black ornamental design for my upper back, inspired by old tarot illustrations. I would like it to feel quiet and detailed, not too dense.
- Style direction: Ornamental, fineline, blackwork.

**Placement and size**

- Body placement: Upper back, centered between the shoulder blades.
- Approximate size: About 15 cm by 12 cm.
- Existing tattoos or scars near the placement: None.

**References**

- Reference image 1: [link]
- Reference image 2: [link]
- Reference image 3: [link]

**Travel window and date flexibility**

- Preferred date range within the trip: April 17 to April 20.
- Are you flexible on dates? Only certain days. I can attend on April 17, 18, or 19. I cannot attend April 20 or later.
- Are you local to the city or traveling to attend? Local. I live in Berlin.

**Studio confirmation**

- I understand the appointment will take place at the studio mentioned in your booking link: Yes.
- Travel logistics: None on my side, since I am local.

**Budget and deposit**

- Approximate budget range: 600 to 800 EUR.
- I have read and accept the deposit policy: Yes.

**Confirmation**

- 18 or older: Yes.
- I can attend during the trip dates: Yes.
- Details are accurate: Yes.

---

## Why this is a strong guest spot request

- The city matches the trip exactly.
- The preferred date range is inside the trip window.
- The client is specific about which days they can attend, which makes scheduling easier.
- Local context is included (the client lives in Berlin, not traveling to the studio).
- Budget aligns with the scope of the design.
- The placement is specific enough to plan around.

A request like this is straightforward to approve, schedule, and follow up on.
