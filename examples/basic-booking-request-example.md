# Basic Booking Request Example

An example of a complete, well-formed tattoo booking request. This is what artists hope to receive on their first read.

The client below is fictional. Reuse the structure, not the names.

---

## Submitted request

**Contact details**

- Name or preferred handle: Mia R
- Instagram handle: @mia.r.designs
- Email: mia.r@example.com

**Tattoo idea**

- In your own words: A small black and grey moth on the inside of my left forearm, somewhere between my wrist and elbow. I would like it to look soft and detailed, with shading rather than heavy lines.
- Style direction: Realism with light dotwork on the shading.

**Placement and size**

- Body placement: Left forearm, inner side, halfway between wrist and elbow.
- Approximate size: About 8 cm by 6 cm.
- Existing tattoos or scars near the placement: A small line tattoo on the wrist, about 6 cm below the planned placement. Photo attached.

**References**

- Reference image 1: [pinterest link]
- Reference image 2: [pinterest link]
- Reference image 3: [photo of the wrist tattoo]
- References to avoid: Color work, heavy black fills.

**Availability**

- Preferred date window: April 8 to April 25.
- Days of the week that work best: Thursdays and Fridays.
- Time of day that works best: Afternoon.
- Dates I cannot do: April 14 to 16 (work trip).

**Budget and deposit**

- Approximate budget range: 250 to 350 EUR.
- I have read and accept the deposit policy: Yes.

**Extra notes**

- I have allergies to latex gloves. I bring my own nitrile gloves to every session.
- This is my third tattoo overall.

**Confirmation**

- 18 or older: Yes.
- I will arrive prepared: Yes.
- Details are accurate: Yes.

---

## Why this is a good request

- The idea is described in the client's own words and is clear enough for the artist to picture immediately.
- Placement and size are specific, not vague.
- References are attached as part of the request, not promised in a follow-up.
- The date window is realistic and includes constraints.
- The budget signals that the project is within reach for the artist's pricing.
- The relevant medical info (latex allergy) is mentioned without dramatizing it.

This kind of request takes a couple of minutes to read and a couple of minutes to decide on. That is the goal.
