# Large Multi-Session Project Request Example

An example of a well-formed request for a big piece built over several sittings, not a single appointment. The two other examples in this repo are small pieces; this one shows what a good request looks like when the work is a months-long project.

The client below is fictional. Reuse the structure, not the names.

---

## Submitted request

**Contact details**

- Name or preferred handle: Devin O
- Instagram handle: @devin.makes
- Email: devin.o@example.com

**Tattoo idea**

- In your own words: A full back piece, ornamental blackwork built around a central botanical motif, flowing out toward the shoulders and down to the lower back. I know this is several sessions. I care more about getting one artist to design the whole back than about finishing fast.
- Style direction: Blackwork and ornamental, heavy black with negative space. No color, no realism.

**Placement and size**

- Body placement: Full back, shoulders to lower back.
- Approximate size: Full back piece.
- Existing tattoos or scars near the placement: One old piece on the right shoulder blade, roughly palm sized. I am open to working around it or covering it, your call. Photo attached.

**References**

- Reference image 1: [pinterest board link]
- Reference image 2: [pinterest board link]
- Reference image 3: [photo of the existing shoulder piece]
- References to avoid: Fine line, mandala-only designs, anything symmetrical to the millimeter.

**The bigger picture**

- Long-term vision: One cohesive back piece designed as a whole, then tattooed in stages.
- Sessions I expect: I assume four to six, and I would rather you tell me than promise a number now.
- Cadence that suits me: One session every four to six weeks, so each part heals before the next.

**Availability**

- Preferred start window: Any time in the next three months.
- Days of the week that work best: Weekends, and I can take Fridays off for a longer sitting.
- Time of day that works best: Full days are fine, I would rather do long sessions than many short ones.
- Dates I cannot do: A two-week trip in July, exact dates to confirm once booked.

**Budget and deposit**

- Approximate budget range: I understand a project like this runs into the low thousands over its life. I can pay per session and can put down a deposit for each.
- I have read and accept the deposit policy: Yes.

**Extra notes**

- This is a long-term commitment for me and I am not shopping around. If you take it on, I am yours for the duration.
- I take blood thinners for a heart condition. My doctor is fine with tattooing; I mention it so we can plan session length and aftercare around it.
- This is my second tattoo. The shoulder piece is the first.

**Confirmation**

- 18 or older: Yes.
- I will arrive prepared: Yes.
- Details are accurate: Yes.

---

## Why this is a good request for a large project

- It names the scope honestly. The client knows it is several sessions and is not asking for a full back in a day.
- The vision is stated before the schedule, so the artist can design the whole back first and stage it second.
- It leaves the session count to the artist instead of demanding a fixed number up front.
- Cadence and availability suit a long project: healing gaps between sittings, and long days rather than many short ones.
- The budget is understood at project scale and accepts per-session deposits, which is how most big pieces are actually paid.
- A genuinely relevant medical detail (blood thinners) is raised as a planning input, not a drama, with the doctor already consulted.

A request like this lets an artist say yes to the whole project with a clear picture of what they are committing to, which is exactly what a big piece needs before the first line goes in.
