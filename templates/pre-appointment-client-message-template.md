# Pre-Appointment Client Message Template

Intended reader: tattoo artists sending practical details to a confirmed client before the appointment.

A clear message a few days before the appointment should answer the practical questions before the client is on the way to the studio. It should repeat the confirmed booking, explain how to arrive prepared, and say what to do if something has changed.

This is a starting template, not medical advice. Adjust it to your studio rules, the placement, the session length, and any guidance from your local health authority. Never tell a client to stop prescribed medication. Send medical questions to an appropriate clinician.

## Full prep message

```text
Hey ___, your tattoo appointment is coming up. Here are the details and prep
notes in one place.

APPOINTMENT
Date: ___
Arrival time: ___
Expected session length: ___
Studio: ___
Address: ___
Entry or parking instructions: ___

TATTOO
Project or design: ___
Placement: ___
Approximate size: ___

MONEY
Deposit received: ___
Estimated remaining balance: ___
Payment methods accepted on the day: ___

BEFORE YOU COME
- Eat normally before the appointment unless a qualified professional has told
  you otherwise.
- Rest, hydrate, and bring a drink or snack if the session is long.
- Wear clean, comfortable clothing that gives easy access to the placement.
- Follow the skin-prep instructions I gave you: ___.
- Do not arrive intoxicated.
- Bring the ID or paperwork required by the studio: ___.

Please contact me before traveling if the skin around the placement is sunburned,
injured, irritated, or otherwise not in its normal condition, or if you are ill.
Do not try to diagnose the issue in a DM. We may need to assess whether the
appointment can go ahead under studio policy.

AT THE STUDIO
Guest policy: ___
Accessibility or privacy notes: ___
What the studio provides: ___
What you should bring: ___

CHANGES
Rescheduling and cancellation policy: ___
Notice deadline: ___
Best contact method for a day-of problem: ___

Please reply by ___ to confirm that you have read this and that the appointment
details are correct. See you soon.
```

## Short reminder

Use this the day before or the morning of the appointment when the full prep message was already sent.

```text
Quick reminder for your tattoo appointment:

Date and arrival time: ___
Studio and address: ___
Project and placement: ___
Estimated remaining balance: ___
Payment methods: ___

Please arrive rested, fed, hydrated, and in clothing that gives easy access to
the placement. Bring ___. If you are ill or the skin at the placement is not in
its normal condition, contact me before traveling: ___.
```

## Flash day version

```text
Your flash appointment is confirmed.

Design ID: ___
Placement and agreed size: ___
Date and arrival time: ___
Studio and address: ___
Deposit received: ___
Estimated balance: ___

Please arrive ___ minutes before your slot with ___. Wear clothing that gives
easy access to the placement. Flash-day slots run tightly, so contact me through
___ as soon as possible if you are delayed.

Guest, lateness, reschedule, and cancellation rules: ___
```

## Guest spot version

```text
Your guest spot appointment in ___ is coming up.

Date and local time: ___
Studio: ___
Full address: ___
Entry instructions: ___
Artist contact while traveling: ___

Project: ___
Placement and size: ___
Deposit received: ___
Estimated balance and accepted payment methods: ___

Please double-check the city, travel time, and local time zone before leaving.
If your plans change, contact me through ___ under the policy here: ___.
```

Repeat the city even when it feels obvious. Traveling artists and clients can both have several trips in motion.

## Placement and clothing notes

Choose only the line that fits the appointment and rewrite it in your voice.

- **Arm or shoulder:** Wear a loose sleeveless or short-sleeve top that can move away from the area.
- **Leg or thigh:** Wear loose shorts or clothing that can expose the placement without pulling across it.
- **Ribs, sternum, chest, or back:** Discuss privacy, coverage, and suitable clothing before the client arrives.
- **Foot or ankle:** Bring footwear that will not press tightly on the fresh placement after the session.
- **Long session:** Bring layers because studio temperature can feel different as the session goes on.

Do not ask a client to undress more than the placement requires. Explain privacy options and studio arrangements before the appointment.

## What not to put in a generic prep message

- Instructions to stop prescribed medication
- A promise that a health or skin concern is safe
- Legal waiver wording that belongs in the studio's consent process
- A new deposit or cancellation rule the client did not agree to
- A different time, address, or balance than the confirmed booking
- Sensitive client details in a group message
- Long aftercare instructions meant for after the tattoo

If the client raises a health question you cannot answer safely, pause the routine message and direct them to an appropriate medical professional and your studio's policy.

## Customize before saving

- Replace every `___` placeholder
- Match the timing to when you actually send reminders
- Keep one current studio address and entry note
- Add your real guest and accessibility policies
- Add only the prep directions you genuinely require
- Link the same reschedule and cancellation policy agreed at booking
- Make the reply channel obvious
- Keep a separate version for guest spots and flash days if those details change

## Send checklist

- [ ] Date, time, studio, and address match the confirmed booking
- [ ] Project, placement, and size match the approved request
- [ ] Deposit and estimated balance are accurate
- [ ] Accepted day-of payment methods are stated
- [ ] Clothing and placement access are covered respectfully
- [ ] The message does not tell the client to stop medication or diagnose a condition
- [ ] Illness or skin concerns have a clear contact step
- [ ] Guest, lateness, reschedule, and cancellation rules match the agreed policy
- [ ] Day-of contact method is easy to find
- [ ] Guest spot messages repeat the city and local time
- [ ] The client is asked to confirm receipt when needed
