# Tattoo Waitlist Signup Template

A reusable Markdown template for a tattoo waitlist signup form. Use it when your books are closed: instead of a dead "books closed, check back later" message, the waitlist keeps collecting demand so you open your next booking window to a ready list of clients. Copy into your form builder, link-in-bio tool, or Notion page.

A waitlist signup asks for much less than a booking request on purpose. The client is not booking anything yet; they are raising a hand. Keep it short enough to fill out in under a minute, and collect the one detail that matters most for planning: the city. For traveling artists, waitlist cities are a free map of where your next guest spots should be.

This template pairs with the [tattoo client intake template](tattoo-client-intake-template.md), which is what waitlisted clients fill out once your books reopen.
