# Books-Open and Books-Closed Announcement Templates

Announcing when your books open and close is a small skill that saves a lot of DMs. Say it once, clearly, with a single link to act on, and the steady drip of "are you taking bookings?" messages mostly stops. Below are copy-paste announcements for the four moments that matter: books opening, closing soon, fully closed, and the waitlist you keep running in between. Replace the bracketed parts with your own.

When your books are closed, the announcement should still give people something to do. That is what the [tattoo waitlist](../docs/tattoo-waitlist-guide.md) is for, and several templates here point at it.

## Books are open

**Instagram caption**

> Books are open. I am taking new requests for [insert month or window, for example April and May] in [insert city or studio]. Send your idea through the form in my bio: placement, size, references, and rough dates. I read each one and reply. Please do not send booking details by DM, they get lost. Link in bio: [insert link].

**Instagram story (short)**

> Books open. Requests through the link, not the DMs. [insert link or sticker]

**Email or newsletter**

> Subject: Books are open for [insert window]
>
> My books just opened for [insert window]. If you have been waiting, now is the time to send your request. Use the form here: [insert link]. Include your idea, placement, size, references, and the dates that work for you. I read every request and reply, usually within [insert timeframe]. Spots are limited and I book in the order strong requests arrive.

**Booking-page banner**

> Books are open for [insert window]. Send a request below.

## Books closing soon

**Instagram caption or story (last call)**

> Last call: books close [insert date]. If you want a spot for [insert window], get your request in before then. After that I am closing requests to work through what is booked. Link in bio: [insert link].

## Books are closed

**Instagram caption**

> Books are closed for now, and thank you for all the requests. I am not taking new bookings until [insert date, or "I reopen, follow for the announcement"]. If you want first access when I reopen, join the waitlist here: [insert waitlist link]. It takes a minute, and you will hear from me before I post publicly.

**Instagram story (short)**

> Books closed for now. Join the waitlist for first access when I reopen: [insert link]

**Booking-page banner**

> Books are closed. Join the waitlist and you will be first to hear when they reopen: [insert waitlist link].

## Waitlist open (evergreen, while books are closed)

**Instagram caption**

> My books are closed, but the waitlist is open. If you have an idea you want me to tattoo, add it to the list: [insert waitlist link]. When I reopen, the waitlist hears first, before any public post. No payment and no commitment, just your idea and your city so I know where demand is.

## How to use these

- Post the open announcement once, clearly, then pin it or keep it in your highlights so latecomers still find it.
- Always give one action and one link. "DM me" scatters the requests you are trying to organize.
- Put a close date on an open announcement. Open-ended books fill unevenly and never let you plan.
- Announce the close too, not just the open. Silence reads as "still open" and the DMs keep coming.
- When closed, point people at the waitlist so the demand does not evaporate.

## Common mistakes

- "DM me to book." It turns your inbox into the booking system the form was meant to replace.
- Opening books with no close date, so you cannot tell when to stop.
- Never announcing the close, so requests keep arriving after you stopped reading them.
- Closing with a dead end instead of a waitlist, so months of interest disappear.
- Different windows on different platforms. Say the same dates everywhere.
