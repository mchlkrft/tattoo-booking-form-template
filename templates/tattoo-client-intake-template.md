# Tattoo Client Intake Template

A reusable tattoo client intake form template in Markdown for freelance and traveling tattoo artists. Copy and paste into your form builder, internal CRM, or Notion page. Adjust field labels to fit your voice.

This template covers the request only. Deposit collection, calendar booking, and confirmation happen after the artist approves the request.
