# Instagram Bio and Booking Reply Copy Pack

Intended reader: Instagram-first tattoo artists who want one clear route from interest to a complete tattoo request.

Short copy for moving serious tattoo requests from an Instagram bio, story, highlight, or DM reply into one clear request path. These lines are routing copy, not full books-open announcements. Replace every `___` placeholder and keep the final version in your own voice.

The rule is simple: one booking state, one action, one link.

## Bio copy: books open

**Direct**

> Tattoo requests open for ___. Use the booking link below.

**Warmer**

> Got an idea for ___? Send the full tattoo request through my booking link.

**Request-first**

> Booking requests: idea, placement, size, and references through the link below.

**No DM bookings**

> Tattoo questions welcome in DMs. Booking requests go through the link.

**Limited window**

> ___ bookings open until ___. Send your request below.

## Bio copy: books closed

**Waitlist open**

> Books closed. Join the waitlist below for the next opening.

**No reopening date yet**

> Books closed for now. Join the waitlist and I will send the next opening there first.

**Travel demand**

> Books closed. Join the city waitlist if you want me to tattoo near you.

Do not leave an old "books open" line in the bio after requests close. The shortest copy only works when it is current.

## Bio copy: guest spots

**One city**

> ___ guest spot, ___ to ___. Requests through the link below.

**Several cities**

> Guest spots: ___ / ___ / ___. Pick your city in the booking link.

**Trip waitlist**

> ___ guest spot full. Join the city waitlist for cancellations or the next trip.

**Traveling artist**

> Tattooing in ___ and ___. Current dates and requests below.

Use the confirmed city and date range. A vague plane emoji and "soon" will create questions, not usable requests.

## Bio copy: flash day

**Requests open**

> Flash day: ___ at ___. Request a design through the link.

**Sheet live**

> New flash is live. Check availability and request your design below.

**Walk-in event**

> Flash walk-ins: ___, ___ to ___. Details and available designs below.

**Fully claimed**

> Flash day is full. Join the cancellation list below.

## Story copy

Use one or two frames before the link sticker. Do not make clients read a full policy in story text.

### Evergreen booking story

**Frame 1**

> Have a tattoo idea?

**Frame 2**

> Send the idea, placement, size, references, and dates through the request link. I review everything before offering a spot.

**Link sticker**

> Send tattoo request

### Guest spot story

**Frame 1**

> ___ guest spot
> ___ to ___ at ___

**Frame 2**

> Want a spot? Send the full request through the link. Please do not split the details across DMs.

**Link sticker**

> Request ___ guest spot

### Books-open and books-closed stories

Use the [books-open and books-closed announcement templates](books-open-announcement-templates.md) for opening, closing soon, closed-books, and waitlist story copy. Keeping those states in one pack prevents the date or action from drifting between versions.

## Highlight names and opening slides

Keep highlight names short enough to scan.

| Highlight | Opening slide copy |
|---|---|
| Bookings | Start here for current booking dates and the request link. |
| Guest spots | Cities, travel dates, and guest spot request links. |
| Flash | Current flash sheets, claim rules, and availability. |
| Waitlist | Books closed? Join here for the next opening. |
| FAQ | Pricing approach, deposits, studio, and what happens after a request. |

If the information changes often, point the highlight to one current page rather than adding ten slides with conflicting dates.

## Saved DM replies

### General booking question

```text
Hey, thanks for reaching out. Send the full tattoo request through the booking
link in my bio: idea, placement, rough size, references, and the dates that work
for you. I review every request before offering a spot.
```

### Price-only message

```text
Hey, I need the idea, placement, size, and references before I can give you a
useful price or range. Send those through the booking link in my bio and I will
review it properly.
```

### Reference image with no context

```text
Thanks for sending this. Add it to the full request through my booking link and
tell me what you like about the reference. Please include the placement, rough
size, and timing too so I can review everything together.
```

### Guest spot question

```text
Yes, I am taking requests for ___ from ___ to ___. Use the guest spot option in
my booking link so the city, dates, idea, placement, size, and references stay
together. I will reply after I review the request.
```

### Books closed

```text
My books are closed right now, so I am not reviewing new booking requests. You
can join the waitlist through the link in my bio. That is not a booking and no
payment is needed. I will contact the list when the next round opens.
```

### Flash claim in a DM

```text
Thanks for the claim. Send the design ID, placement, size, and backup choice
through the flash request link in my bio. The design is confirmed only after I
approve the request and send the confirmation step.
```

## Link sticker labels

- Send tattoo request
- Start booking request
- Request this guest spot
- Pick your city
- View flash and request
- Join the waitlist
- Read booking details
- Check current dates

Use a verb that matches the state. "Book now" is misleading when the client is only submitting a request for artist review.

## Pinned post footer

Add this to a booking explainer or portfolio post you keep pinned:

```text
Want to work together? Use the booking link in my bio. Send the idea, placement,
rough size, references, and the dates that work for you. I review every request
before I offer an appointment, so submitting the form does not lock a time.
```

## Copy check before publishing

- [ ] The current state is clear: open, closed, guest spot, flash, or waitlist
- [ ] The city and date window appear when they matter
- [ ] There is one action and one link
- [ ] The copy says request when the artist still needs to approve
- [ ] The same booking state appears in the bio, stories, and pinned content
- [ ] The DM reply asks clients to use the link instead of repeating the full intake
- [ ] Old dates and expired guest spots have been removed
- [ ] The final wording sounds like the artist who will reply
