# Tattoo Deposit Policy Template

A tattoo deposit policy is one of the most important documents in a tattoo artist's booking flow. It sets expectations on what the deposit covers, what happens if a client reschedules or cancels, and how no-shows are handled. This file is a starting template, not a contract. Adapt it to your local laws, your studio's rules, and your own workflow.

## Important disclaimer

This template does not constitute legal advice. Deposit and cancellation rules are affected by consumer protection law, regional tattoo regulations, and studio policy. Have a local lawyer or your studio manager review the final wording before publishing it on your booking page or website.

## What a tattoo deposit policy should cover

- The deposit amount or percentage
- What the deposit reserves
- Rescheduling rules
- Cancellation rules
- No-show rules
- Design change rules
- Refund conditions
- Payment methods accepted
- Confirmation wording the client sees

## Starter template

You can copy the section below into your booking page or studio FAQ.

---

### Deposit policy

To confirm a tattoo appointment, a deposit is required. The deposit:

- Reserves your appointment slot on the calendar
- Covers preparation time, including consultation, design work, and pre-session admin
- Is applied to the final session cost on the day of the appointment

### Deposit amount

A standard deposit is [insert amount or percentage]. Larger projects or multi-session pieces may require a higher deposit.

### Rescheduling

If you need to reschedule:

- More than [insert window, for example 48 hours] before the appointment: the deposit is transferred to the new date at no extra cost.
- Less than [insert window] before the appointment: the deposit is forfeited and a new deposit is required to rebook.
- Rescheduling is allowed [insert number, for example once or twice] per booking before a new deposit is required.

### Cancellations

If you cancel:

- More than [insert window, for example 7 days] before the appointment: the deposit is non-refundable but may be applied to a future booking within [insert window, for example 6 months].
- Less than [insert window] before the appointment: the deposit is forfeited.

### No-shows

If you do not arrive within [insert window, for example 30 minutes] of the appointment start without contacting the artist, the appointment is considered a no-show. The deposit is forfeited and a new booking and deposit will be required for any future session.

### Design changes

The deposit covers the design that was discussed during the request and any agreed revisions before the appointment. Significant changes on the day of the appointment may require a new deposit, especially when:

- The placement changes
- The size changes by more than [insert percentage, for example 25 percent]
- The style or subject changes substantially

### Refund conditions

Deposits are generally non-refundable. Exceptions may include:

- The artist needs to cancel the appointment and cannot reschedule
- Medical or legal reasons documented within [insert window]

Decisions are made case by case and are at the artist's discretion.

### Payment method

Deposits are paid through [insert your method, for example Stripe, bank transfer, in-person]. The deposit confirmation includes a receipt.

### Booking confirmation wording

After the deposit is received, the client receives a message that reads something like:

> Your deposit has been received. Your appointment on [date] is confirmed. Please review the deposit policy attached. If you need to reschedule, contact me at least [window] before your appointment.

---

## Checklist before publishing your policy

- [ ] Deposit amount or percentage is clearly stated
- [ ] Rescheduling window is specific (not "advance notice")
- [ ] Cancellation window is specific
- [ ] No-show rule is clear
- [ ] Refund conditions are spelled out
- [ ] Payment methods are listed
- [ ] Local laws have been checked or reviewed by a lawyer
- [ ] The policy is linked from the booking form
