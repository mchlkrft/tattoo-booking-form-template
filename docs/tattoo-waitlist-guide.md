# Tattoo Waitlist Guide: Collect Bookings While Your Books Are Closed

Closing your books should not mean losing clients. Every week your booking page says "closed, check back later", interested clients bounce, forget, or book someone else. A tattoo artist waitlist fixes that: a tiny signup form that keeps collecting demand while you are heads-down, traveling, or fully booked, so the next time you open, you start from a list instead of from zero.

This guide covers when to use a waitlist, what to ask, how to manage the list, and how traveling artists can turn waitlist cities into a guest spot plan. The matching form is in [templates/tattoo-waitlist-signup-template.md](../templates/tattoo-waitlist-signup-template.md).

## When a waitlist beats closed books

- You close books for months at a time and reopen in batches
- You are mid-move, mid-tour, or on parental leave but want momentum waiting for you
- You cap monthly bookings and regularly hit the cap
- You travel and want to know which city wants you next

If you take requests year-round and reply within days, you do not need a waitlist; you need a booking form. The waitlist is specifically for the closed state.

## What a waitlist signup should ask

Less than you think. A waitlist signup is not a booking request: nobody commits, nobody pays, and over-asking kills signups. The useful minimum:

| Field | Why |
|---|---|
| Name or handle | Who is raising their hand |
| Email | The only reliable reopening channel you own |
| Rough idea (1-2 sentences) | Lets you gauge fit before inviting |
| City | The single highest-value field, see below |
| Flexibility note | Separates "anytime" clients from "only March" clients |

Leave references, placement, size, and budget for the real booking form later. Asking for them twice annoys clients and makes the eventual requests staler.

## The city field is a planning tool

For artists who travel, the waitlist doubles as market research that costs nothing. Group signups by city:

- Five names in Berlin and two in Hamburg is an itinerary, not trivia
- A city you have never worked showing repeat signups is a guest spot lead
- Your home city dominating the list means reopening locally beats touring

Sort and count by city every time you plan travel. Clients told you where to go; most artists never look.

## Working the list when books reopen

1. **Email the waitlist first.** Before the Instagram announcement, before the story countdown. Early access is the entire reward for signing up, and it trains future clients to join the list.
2. **Send a structured booking form, not "DM me".** The waitlist holds interest; the booking form holds details. Linking the form keeps the reopening wave organized instead of recreating DM chaos at 10x volume.
3. **Give the list a head start, not an exclusive.** A 24 to 72 hour window before public opening works well. Waitlisted clients feel valued; everyone else still gets a fair shot.
4. **Prune as you go.** Mark entries that converted into bookings, and drop entries that went quiet after two reopenings. A stale waitlist produces stale expectations about demand.

## Common mistakes

- **Treating the waitlist as a queue.** Signup order is not booking order. You still review fit, size, and schedule like any other request. Say so in the signup form's expectations section.
- **Taking deposits at signup.** Money before review creates obligations you have not agreed to. The deposit belongs after you accept the actual booking request; see the [tattoo deposit policy template](tattoo-deposit-policy-template.md).
- **Collecting signups in DMs.** A "DM me to get on the list" waitlist is a screenshot folder. The point of the list is structure: one place, consistent fields, countable cities.
- **Never reopening.** A waitlist is a promise of eventual access. If books stay closed for a year with no word, send one honest update email rather than letting the list rot.

## Waitlist checklist

- [ ] Signup form is short (under one minute to complete)
- [ ] City field is included and required
- [ ] Expectations state it is not a booking and no payment is taken
- [ ] Reopening emails link a structured booking form
- [ ] The list gets pruned after each reopening
- [ ] City counts feed your travel planning
